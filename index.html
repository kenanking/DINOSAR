Large-Scale SAR Self-Supervised Pretraining
with Content-Aware View Construction

Yan Tang*, Yifeng Jin*, Zekai Miao, Min Ji, Yu Duan, Shaoming Zhang, and Jianmei Wang

* Equal contribution

Submitted to the International Journal of Digital Earth (IJDE) · 2026

DINOSAR learns transferable SAR representations by constructing views around informative scattering structures. Content-aware multi-crop (CAMC) combines structural content, scene-adaptive anchors, and coverage diversity within a self-distillation framework.

  • Content-aware views

    CAMC constructs pretraining crops around informative scattering structures instead of random sampling — +7.98 pp frozen k-NN over random crops in matched ablations.

01 · Method

Content-aware view construction

CAMC guides global and local views toward informative SAR scattering structures, while retaining the DINO, iBOT, and KoLeo training objectives.

Why can random crops fail in SAR?

DINO-style self-distillation aligns representations across global and local views. Its supervision is more meaningful when these views share informative image content.

In target-sparse SAR scenes, informative scattering structures are sparse and unevenly distributed. Random local crops can capture mostly background clutter—as illustrated by L2 and L3—providing limited target-related evidence for alignment with the global views.

CAMC makes view construction content-aware. Structural content and scene-adaptive anchors guide crop placement, while coverage diversity discourages redundant local views, balancing informative content with spatial diversity.

Inside CAMC

From SAR structure to informative views

A per-image, non-learned content prior guides crop construction. The prior selects views; it is not a reconstruction target.

  1. 01 · Content prior

    Locate structural content

    Compute local mean-ratio gradients at scales 3 and 7. Weight responses by local mean intensity, fuse the scales, and normalize into the content map \(T\).

    Anchor supports \(\mathcal A\)
    Retain prominent peaks with non-maximum suppression; use a central fallback if no reliable peak is found.
    Scene concentration \(c\)
    Measure the spatial compactness of the content distribution: \(c=1-\sigma^2/2\).

    Outputs: content map · anchors · concentration

  2. 02 · Global views

    Choose a shared or split support

    The normalized diagonal span of the anchor union determines the layout: \(\rho=d(U(\mathcal A))/d(I)\).

    \(\rho<0.7\)Two scales around the same anchor union.
    \(\rho\geq0.7\)Two anchor clusters; one view per cluster.

    Dispersed anchors are partitioned using farthest-point initialization. Both layouts retain surrounding context.

    Output: two global views and their covered support

  3. 03 · Local views

    Balance content, focus, and diversity

    Propose candidate boxes inside the global-view union, biasing their centers toward structural content while varying size, aspect ratio, and position.

    \(q_i(b)=\bar T(b)\,A(b)^c\,U_i(b)^{1-c}\)
    \(\bar T(b)\) · Content
    Mean structural response inside the candidate.
    \(A(b)^c\) · Anchor proximity
    Higher concentration strengthens focus around anchor supports.
    \(U_i(b)^{1-c}\) · Coverage diversity
    Lower concentration strengthens the reward for covering new regions.

    Greedily select the highest-scoring box, update coverage, and suppress excessive overlap. Repeat for six local views.

    Output: six complementary local views

Two controls, different roles: anchor span \(\rho\) selects the global layout; concentration \(c\) continuously balances local focus and diversity. A uniform spatial distribution has reference concentration \(c\approx2/3\), not zero.

DINOSAR pretraining framework

CAMC supplies the global and local views for teacher–student self-distillation. The EMA teacher processes global views, while the student processes both global and local views. Training combines image-level DINO distillation, patch-level iBOT distillation, and KoLeo regularization to learn transferable SAR representations.

Single-channel input · four register tokens · EMA teacher · AdamW with cosine schedules · effective batch size 1,280 (ViT-S) / 1,536 (ViT-B)

A corpus of 7,047,666 single-channel SAR images assembled from five public datasets and openly released imagery from Capella, ICEYE, and Umbra.

Multiple sources, consistent preparation

The public sources comprise SARDet-100K, ATRNet-STAR, FAIR-CSAR, FUSAR-Ship, and AIR-PolSAR-Seg-2.0. The commercial component contains tiles from 2,274 scenes.

Commercial scenes are tiled into non-overlapping 256 × 256 patches, followed by NoData filtering, background filtering, and embedding-based deduplication within each source. Public datasets use source-specific processing.
